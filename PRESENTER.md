# Finclus — Intelligence Fabric
## Presenter guide | 60-minute live demo

Audience: Indian banking leadership, RBI officials, bank CXOs and Chief Credit Officers, TSPs, and fintech founders.

This is a scripted workshop demo using the synthetic Sri Annapurna Foods Private Limited case. It is not a live RBIH, ULI, or AA integration. The top strip must remain visible: **SIMULATED — WORKSHOP DEMO · synthetic borrower · no live RBIH/ULI/AA call · every fact is sourced and labelled**.

Keep the central thesis in view: the core records what happened; the intelligence fabric understands what is happening, why, and what to investigate; the human decides.

### Pace rules

- Keep the anchor case intact from the borrower journey through sanction; do not introduce a second enterprise case.
- The ₹5.00 Cr case is the vehicle. The rural cluster and ₹1.60 lakh dairy case are the payload.
- If a discussion runs long, protect the four WOW moments, the cluster, Lakshmi, governance, and the 90-day ask. Move quickly through the frame screens first.
- Never claim a live external integration, an unlabelled measured TAT, a measured 100× gain, or an AI-only credit decision.
- If challenged on a number, press `G` or `F`; use the fact registry rather than improvising.

## Four WOW Moments

### WOW 1 — Refuse to guess

Route: `/bank/conflict/03417` | Minute 38

1. Show audited FY25 turnover ₹6.90 Cr, GSTN GSTR-3B ₹8.20 Cr, and AA bank credits ₹7.80 Cr.
2. Point to the explicit `18.8% > 10% tolerance` variance.
3. Do not click through the discrepancy or offer a blended number.
4. Ask Question 2, then click **Route to credit review**.

**Exact cue line:** “I cannot safely resolve this discrepancy. Three sources disagree by 18.8%, above the 10% policy tolerance. This requires a credit officer.”

**Point to make:** A safe system preserves uncertainty and routes judgment. It does not turn conflicting evidence into false precision.

### WOW 2 — Retract a confident mistake

Route: `/bank/correction/03417` | Minute 40

1. Let the fabric show confidence `0.91`: land parcel 214/2B is industrial and conversion is complete.
2. Click **Challenge**.
3. Select **Field inspection contradicts this** and attach the RM site note.
4. Let retrieval find the later revenue record dated 2 Jun 2026.
5. Open the governance drawer and show the original claim, stale source, challenger, corrected claim, downstream CP change, and event ID.

**Exact cue line:** “Retracted. My source was stale by 80 days. Land-use conversion is pending, not complete. Confidence in the original claim: withdrawn. This changes CP #3 and delays the subsidy claim.”

**Point to make:** The objective is not an AI that never makes mistakes. It is a lending system where mistakes are detectable, explainable, and correctable before they become losses.

### WOW 3 — See risk before default

Route: `/portfolio/ews/03417` | Minute 48

1. Start with the split: traditional LMS says `CURRENT · 0 DPD · nothing to see`; the fabric says `EARLY WARNING`.
2. Reveal all five signals one by one.
3. For each signal, show the specific sanction-time assumption it violates.
4. State explicitly that this is not an NPA and not a default; it is an intervention recommendation at SMA-0.

**Exact cue line:** “This is not an NPA and not a default. It is intelligence before default, at SMA-0.”

**Point to make:** Monitoring becomes useful when a new signal is reconciled to the original credit reasoning, not merely added to an alert queue.

### WOW 4 — Turn one sanction into a rural cluster

Route: `/portfolio/cluster` | Minute 51

1. Start at the sanctioned Annapurna unit and say that the same evidence can now be reused.
2. Let 312 procurement suppliers resolve across Kuppam, Gudupalle, Ramakuppam, and Santhipuram.
3. Reveal `287 of 312`, `₹4.68 Cr`, `₹1.63 lakh`, `196 new-to-credit`, and `171 women`.
4. Pause on the 25 that cannot be evidenced: no digitised land record, no consent, or tenant cultivator without record of rights.
5. Show the PSL and collateral framing, then deliver the exact close.

**Exact cue line:** “One MSME sanction did not produce one asset. It produced a village credit cluster the bank already had a claim on and could not see.”

**Point to make:** The fabric is valuable not only because it improves a large-ticket appraisal, but because it changes the arithmetic of small-ticket rural underwriting while being honest about data gaps.

## Top Five Objections

### 1. “What does this cost, and where is the ROI?”

**Answer:** “We do not have a credible public benchmark for fully loaded appraisal cost or MSME sanction TAT, so we label both as illustrative and measure them with the bank. The pilot measures sanction TAT, cost per decision, officer throughput, CAM quality, conflict catches, scheme capture, EWS lead time, and rural borrowers surfaced per anchor sanction. We are proposing one state, one bank, one product, and 90 days. If the measured value is not there, we stop. The objective is more productive bankers and more underwritable borrowers, not fewer bankers.”

Do not claim that 100× is measured. The case shows approximately 7–9× throughput as a simulated observation; the 100× full-workforce figure is a design hypothesis.

### 2. “How is this RBI-compliant?”

**Answer:** “This demo is not a claim of approval or a substitute for the RE’s controls. It is designed around the controls the bank must own: the RE remains responsible for its LSP; material decisions have mandatory human oversight; claims carry provenance and confidence; models and agents are inventoried and validated; customer-facing AI is disclosed; and adversarial testing is explicit. The 2026 RBI MRM guidance is still draft, so we present it as the operative design target, not binding regulation. The Digital Lending Directions support the principle that AI recommends and the human decides.”

Be precise: FREE-AI is a report, not binding regulation. DPDP consent duties shown in the demo are future-timeline aware; the displayed timeline puts the relevant obligations at 12 May 2027. Do not present either as a current blanket compliance claim.

### 3. “How do you prevent hallucination?”

**Answer:** “We do not promise a hallucination-free model. We constrain the architecture: deterministic policy rules are not delegated to an LLM; material assertions carry evidence chips; sources, transformations, consent artefacts, versions, and human authority are visible; conflicts are carried forward rather than smoothed; and officers can challenge a claim. The land-use screen deliberately shows a confident error, retrieves a later source, retracts the claim, writes the audit event, and changes the downstream condition. Reliability comes from detectability, explainability, and correction, not from pretending the model is infallible.”

### 4. “Are you displacing our TSP, LOS, CBS, or LMS?”

**Answer:** “No. This is the layer above the existing stack and is TSP-neutral by construction. LOS, CBS, and LMS remain systems of record and execution. The intelligence fabric reconciles evidence, coordinates agents, explains reasoning, and returns a decision-of-record to the bank’s authority. It should make the existing core intelligent rather than replace it. The RE still owns accountability for its LSP; that is why provenance and exception handling must be product surfaces, not promises.”

### 5. “Rural land records are incomplete. Won’t this create bad loans?”

**Answer:** “Incomplete land records are a reason to show uncertainty, not to manufacture certainty. The cluster explicitly leaves 25 of 312 suppliers unevidenced and says why: no digitised land record, no consent, or tenant cultivator without a record of rights. The system can combine procurement, milk or mandi records, PM-KISAN, land records, and AA data where consent exists, but it cannot create missing proof. It routes unresolved evidence to a human, and it can still identify a 287-borrower evidenceable opportunity without pretending the full 312 are underwritable.”

For Lakshmi, the same discipline applies: a 26-month milk-society history supports a small-ticket recommendation, while the two-month disagreement between pouring and payment ledgers remains a flagged conflict.
